    Playing on Quest
    1. Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser on your headset.
    2. To add maps, download a map zip from BeatSaver in the headset browser, then choose it here.
    3. Press Enter VR. Point at the menu and pull the trigger to choose.
    4. Press B or Y to pause. Run Settings → Calibrate once so the notes match the music.
    Desktop controls
    1. Move the mouse to swing one saber that cuts both colours.
    2. Click to choose in menus. Esc pauses.
